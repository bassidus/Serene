**<span style="color: #f65820;">Serene</span>** is a lightweight quality-of-life suite for WoW Forever. It cleans up the default interface and handles the small chores, without replacing Blizzard's UI.

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Modular and lightweight</span>**  
Turn off the modules you don't want. Settings you turn off cost next to nothing and everything is event based to use as little CPU as possible.

<br>
## <span style="color: #f65820;">Modules</span>
**<span style="color: #f65820;">Action Bars</span>**  
Short keybinds, Out of range coloring and more

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Dark Mode</span>**  
Dark art in place of the bronze and gold on the action bars, unit frames and more…

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Nameplates</span>**  
Flat enemy plates with threat colors, cast bars, debuffs, combo points and more…

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Move & Scale</span>**  
Move and resize Blizzard's windows. Drag to move, Ctrl + scroll to resize.

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Tooltip</span>**  
Customizable position, scale, font and border and more…

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Unit Frames</span>**  
Class colored health bars, hide damage numbers and more…

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Quality of Life</span>**  
Quicker auto-loot, no bind-on-pickup popups, easier deleting of items, adds a Train All button to various trainers, a battleground queue countdown and more…

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Vendors</span>**  
Space over a bag item marks it as junk with a red cross. At vendors it sells all junk, repairs your gear and dims items you already know. The settings are under Quality of Life.

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Quests</span>**  
Auto accept/turn in quests and marks quest enemies on their nameplates with your progress

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Social</span>**  
Automatically accepts group invites, choose who can duel you, announce instance resets, thank players who buff you and more…

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Chat</span>**  
Player levels, shorter messages, clickable web links, right-click to copy and more…

**<span style="color: #f65820;">Other</span>**  
Screenshot and time played on level up, action bars and Edit Mode layout for new characters.

<br>
## <span style="color: #f65820;">Installation & Usage</span>

Install with the CurseForge app, or extract every Serene folder into Interface/AddOns. Serene is the core and is required. All other modules are optional.

<span style="color: #f65820;">Minimap icon</span> left-click opens the options, right-click shows the CPU meter, Shift-right-click reloads the UI.  
<span style="color: #f65820;">/serene</span> opens the options.  
<span style="color: #f65820;">/serene reset</span> resets all settings to default.
