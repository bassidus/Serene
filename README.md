# Serene

Small quality-of-life tweaks for WoW Forever.

![Options window](images/options.png)

Serene is a core addon plus a set of modules, and every module is its own addon. If you don't want one, disable it in the addon list or on the Modules page in the options.

## What's in it

### General
- Faster looting
- Repairs and sells your grays at vendors
- Train All button at trainers
- Skips cinematics, hides tutorials and error spam
- More camera zoom

### Quests
- Accepts and turns in quests for you (hold Shift to do it yourself)
- Marks quest mobs on their nameplates with your progress

### Social
- Accepts group invites from friends
- Blocks invites, duels and shared quests from strangers
- Invites people who whisper "inv"
- Says thanks when someone buffs you

### Action bars
- Grays out and colors abilities when the target is out of range
- Shorter keybind text, macro names, your own fonts

### Unit frames
- Class colored health bars, darker frames
- Name, level and health text the way you want them

![Unit frames](images/unitframes.png)

### Tooltip
- Follows the cursor
- Cleaner look, your own font and size
- Shows target, item level, guild rank and IDs

![Tooltip](images/tooltip.png)

### Enemy nameplates
- Flat plates with threat colors
- Cast bar and debuffs

![Nameplates](images/nameplates.png)

### Move & scale
- Drag Blizzard's windows anywhere
- Ctrl + scroll to make them bigger or smaller

### FPS & latency
- A small readout you can put anywhere

### Performance
- CPU and memory use for every addon

## Install

Copy the `Serene` folders into `Interface/AddOns`. `Serene` is the core and has to be there. The rest are optional.

## Usage

- `/serene` opens the options.
- `/serene reset` sets everything back to default.
- The minimap icon opens the options on left-click and reloads the UI on right-click.
