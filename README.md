# Serene

Lightweight QoL addon for World of Warcraft Forever. Each feature is small and can be toggled on its own.

### Features
- **Quick Loot**: Loots quicker than default Auto Loot.
- **Skip Loot Warnings**: Automatically confirms the "will bind to you" question.
- **Auto-Fill Delete**: Automatically types DELETE into the "delete this good item?" popup.
- **Auto Repair**: Automatically repairs at merchants.
- **Auto Sell Junk**: Automatically sells gray items at merchants.
- **Train All Button**: Learns everything you can train at a trainer.
- **Auto Accept / Turn-In Quests**: Hold Shift to handle a quest yourself.
- **Hide Tutorials**: Hides tutorial pop-ups.
- **Faster Movie Skip**: Escape, Space or Enter skips a movie right away, without confirmation.
- **Max Camera Zoom**: Lets the camera zoom out further.
- **Hide Error Messages**: Hides the "Not enough rage" / "Out of range" warnings.
- **FPS & Latency**: A small on-screen readout you can drag anywhere.

### Social
All off by default.
- **Accept Invites from Friends**: group invites from friends are accepted automatically while you are not in a group.
- **Block Group Invites**, **Block Duels** and **Block Shared Quests**: declines them from anyone who is not a friend, and the chat says who was declined.
- **Guild Counts as Friends**: friends are your friends list and Battle.net friends. With this on, your guild counts too, for all the settings above and for Invite on Whisper.
- **Invite on Whisper**: whispering `inv` / `invite` gets an invite. If you can't invite, the player gets a whisper back saying why.
- **Thank you for the buff**: when a player outside your group buffs you (Fortitude, Mark of the Wild, Arcane Intellect, Blessings...), you say thanks with an `/emote` after a short pause, at most once a minute. Pick a message or let it vary. Buffs received in combat can't be seen by addons on this client and are not thanked.

### Action Bars
- **Range Coloring**: Icons are grayed out and turn the chosen color while the target is out of range.
- **Short Keybind Text** (CTRL-2 → C2, Mouse Button 4 → M4), **Hide Macro Text**, plus keybind font, size, outline, color and X/Y offset.

### Unit Frames
- **Class Colored Healthbars**: Class colors on Blizzard's unit frames.
- **Dark Mode**: dark frame art.
- Portrait (portrait, class icon, or hidden together with the frame), hide name, name color (class or any color), hide the colored bar behind the target's name.
- Level in its circle, before the name ("14 Hogger") or hidden.
- Health/mana text as value, percent or both.
- Hide the resting glow and Zzz animation on the player frame.

### Tooltip Customization
- Position (follows the cursor, with offsets), font, size and outline.
- Hide the border and set background opacity.
- Hide the beta "Press F6 to submit an issue" line.
- Hide the "<Right click for Frame Settings>" line on unit frame tooltips.
- Keep the unit tooltip while hovering a unit frame's health or mana bar.
- Content: class, level and faction colors, player titles, guild rank, the unit's target, health bar, item level of items and players, spell/item IDs.

### Nameplates
Flat enemy nameplates: threat / class / reaction colors, level, health percent, target highlight, cast bar, debuffs (everyone's, or only yours) and quest mob markers with progress and an optional bar color. You can set font, size, outline, width and height.

### Performance
A page listing every loaded addon's CPU and memory use (sortable), plus average framerate and latency over the last 5 minutes (loading screens are left out).

## Usage
`/serene` Opens or closes the options window.
The minimap icon opens the options when you left-click it and reloads the UI when you right-click it.
