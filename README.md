**Serene** is a lightweight quality-of-life suite for WoW Forever. It cleans up the default interface and handles the small chores, without replacing Blizzard's UI.

**Modular and lightweight**  
Turn off the modules you don't want. Settings you turn off cost next to nothing and everything is event based to use as little CPU as possible.

## Modules

**Action Bars**  
Short keybinds, Out of range coloring and more

**Dark Mode**  
Dark art in place of the bronze and gold on the action bars, unit frames and more…

**Nameplates**  
Flat enemy plates with threat colors, cast bars, debuffs, combo points and more…

**Move & Scale**  
Move and resize Blizzard's windows. Drag to move, Ctrl + scroll to resize.

**Tooltip**  
Customizable position, scale, font and border and more…

**Unit Frames**  
Class colored health bars, hide damage numbers and more…

**General tweaks**  
Quicker auto-loot, no bind-on-pickup popups, easier deleting of items, auto sell/repair, adds a Train All button to various trainers, a battleground queue countdown and more…

**Quests**  
Auto accept/turn in quests and marks quest enemies on their nameplates with your progress

**Social**  
Automatically accepts group invites, choose who can duel you, announce instance resets and more…

**Chat**  
Player levels, shorter messages, clickable web links, right-click to copy and more…

**Other**  
Screenshot and time played on level up, thank players who buff you, action bars and Edit Mode layout for new characters.

## Installation & Usage

Install with the CurseForge app, or extract every Serene folder into Interface/AddOns. Serene is the core and is required. All other modules are optional.

Minimap icon left-click opens the options, right-click reloads the UI.  
`/serene` opens the options.  
`/serene` reset resets all settings to default.
